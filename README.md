# Hi, I'm Nikolay Mamadaev 👋

**Lead Product Designer · complex B2B and AI interfaces**

I design complex product interfaces — automation systems, AI-powered tools, analytics — where a "pretty screen" doesn't solve the problem, but a well-thought-out experience does. Over the past 10+ years I've turned product hypotheses into clear user scenarios, built design systems, and taken solutions from research all the way to release alongside engineering.

Today most of my focus sits at the intersection of human experience and AI: I design not just the screen, but how a user learns to trust a machine agent. Alongside that I automate whatever I can — n8n workflows, REST APIs, small tools and pet projects that take the routine out of my own work and out of research.

Outside of design I'm into electronics: Arduino and ESP32 boards, sensors, and small homemade devices and prototypes. I like things that exist in the physical world, not only in Figma.

Always open to interesting product problems, contracts, and good conversations.
